# High-Level Project Structure

## Purpose of this view

AICore is a substantial software project organized around a complete product journey: understanding user and application needs, shaping an adaptive response, coordinating the computer experience, preserving trust, and improving relevance over time.

This public view explains the scale and coherence of the project while protecting its proprietary methods.

## Product layers

| Layer | Role in the product | Commercial relevance |
|---|---|---|
| User and application context | Represents what the person and active work are trying to achieve | Creates a more relevant and differentiated customer experience |
| Adaptive intelligence | Interprets changing priorities and identifies an appropriate product response | Supports personalization and premium AI PC positioning |
| Coordination and decision | Brings competing priorities into one coherent device strategy | Creates whole-device value beyond isolated utilities |
| Platform connection | Relates the AICore experience to operating systems and diverse computing resources | Supports expansion across device families and partner ecosystems |
| Trust and control | Keeps the experience understandable, governed, and aligned with user choice | Strengthens enterprise, OEM, and customer confidence |
| Insight and evolution | Uses accumulated context to improve relevance and product value over time | Enables longer-term services and recurring commercial opportunities |
| Experience and presentation | Makes adaptive behavior visible and understandable to users and partners | Supports adoption, differentiation, and branded product experiences |

## Project scope

The private AICore project includes the software asset, product logic, platform strategy, user-facing experience, supporting documentation, brand materials, and commercialization framework needed to present the product as one coherent acquisition opportunity.

## Cross-platform perspective

AICore is designed around a consistent product identity that can extend across Windows and Linux environments and across different classes of personal-computing hardware. Platform diversity is treated as an opportunity for commercial reach rather than a reason to fragment the customer experience.

## Why this structure matters

The project structure demonstrates that AICore is more than a single feature or concept. It is a coordinated product foundation with clear places for customer experience, intelligence, platform partnerships, trust, and commercial expansion.

## Confidential depth

Qualified acquirers and strategic partners can review deeper project materials through a controlled due-diligence process under appropriate confidentiality terms.
