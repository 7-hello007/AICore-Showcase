# Product Framework

![Adaptive intelligence](../assets/adaptive-intelligence.png)

## AICore at a glance

AICore connects three business perspectives: what the user wants to accomplish, what applications need, and how the device can best support the experience. This creates a distinct product layer for adaptive personal computing.

## Experience principles

### Understand context

The product considers changing user priorities, workload characteristics, and device conditions as part of one experience.

### Coordinate the whole device

AICore treats the computer as a unified product rather than a set of disconnected resources and utilities.

### Adapt over time

The experience can become more relevant as usage patterns, applications, and device portfolios evolve.

### Preserve trust

Control, transparency, user choice, and responsible behavior remain central to the product proposition.

## Ecosystem role

AICore is designed to complement operating systems, hardware platforms, applications, and enterprise management. This makes it suitable for inclusion in broader product portfolios without displacing the value of existing ecosystem partners.

## Scalability

The product concept can expand across consumer devices, professional workstations, enterprise fleets, and specialized computing markets while maintaining a consistent AICore identity.

## Strategic outcome

For customers, AICore represents a computer that feels more aware and responsive. For a commercial owner, it represents a reusable platform for differentiation, partnership, and recurring value creation.
