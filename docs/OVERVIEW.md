# Product Overview

![Premium AI PC experience](../assets/premium-ai-pc.png)

## Vision

AICore envisions a computer that understands changing priorities and continuously shapes its behavior around the person, application, and task at hand.

## Product category

AICore defines an AI computer control layer: a software product category that brings adaptive intelligence to the whole-device experience. It gives manufacturers, enterprises, and partners a way to turn heterogeneous computing into a coherent customer proposition.

## Problem addressed

The modern personal computer is powerful but fragmented. Users move between communication, creation, analysis, entertainment, and local AI, while device behavior is often divided among fixed modes and separate vendor experiences.

AICore creates a unified layer of product intelligence that helps the computer respond to what the user is trying to achieve.

## Customer outcomes

- A more responsive and context-aware computing experience
- Clear differentiation for AI PC and premium-device portfolios
- Greater value from diverse computing resources
- A consistent experience across product families
- New opportunities for services, licensing, and ecosystem partnerships

## Strategic audiences

| Audience | Strategic opportunity |
|---|---|
| Acquirers | Obtain a differentiated adaptive-computing product and intellectual-property asset |
| OEMs | Add a branded intelligence experience across device portfolios |
| Enterprises | Align device behavior with productivity and organizational priorities |
| Silicon partners | Connect hardware capability with visible customer value |
| Software partners | Create richer experiences informed by device and workload context |

## Product promise

AICore turns computing complexity into an intelligent, coherent, and commercially valuable experience.
